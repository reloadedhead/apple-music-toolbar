# Privacy Policy: Music but with a Toolbar

Last updated: November 2026

Music but with a Toolbar ("the extension") is an independent project by Tomás García Gobet. It is not affiliated with Apple Inc.

## What the extension does
It changes how the Apple Music web player looks on music.apple.com, by adding a stylesheet and two small scripts. One reads the colors of the page's sidebar. The other clicks the player's own volume button so the volume slider stays visible. The scripts only act on the page itself; they do not read your account, your library or what you play.

## Data
The extension does not collect, store, share or send any personal data or usage data. It makes no network requests of its own, has no analytics, and does not use cookies or local storage.

## Permissions
The extension requests no browser permissions. It runs only on https://music.apple.com/* and https://beta.music.apple.com/*.

## Third parties
The extension does not share data with anyone. Apple Music itself is a service provided by Apple Inc., and its own privacy policy applies when you use it.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
